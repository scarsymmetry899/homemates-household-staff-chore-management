# Household sticker setup

## Personal attendance stickers available today

On the owner's NFC-capable phone, open Settings, choose the staff member, and tap **Program**. Write the sticker, lift it, and tap it again to verify its contents. Wait for the registration confirmation. Label this sticker with the person's name and keep it for their attendance using the registering browser. A sticker left by the kitchen door would identify that same person on every scan.

If writing or read-back fails, keep the sticker loose and retry with the same phone and sticker. If a sticker is lost, use manual attendance and contact the owner. The current release has no verified remote disable or replacement workflow.

## Placement for the planned staff-phone release

Mount shared stickers only after a staff member's own signed-in phone successfully resolves each location and the owner sees the resulting record. Use removable mounting during testing.

| Location | Printed label | Staff action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inside the staff entrance | Attendance | Choose Check in or Check out and check the saved time. |
| Dry surface beside the kitchen work area | Kitchen tasks | Select the assigned breakfast, dishes, or cleaning task. |
| Beside the laundry supplies | Laundry tasks | Choose washing or drying separately. |
| Near the garden exit or pet supplies | Garden or pet care | Choose the assigned watering or walking task. |

For acceptance, scan with two different staff accounts. Check the person, task, room, and time from the owner's second session. Test a repeated scan, a disabled sticker, a sticker from another household, denied permission, and a disconnected phone before permanent mounting.
